# Model Validation and Hypothesis Testing

This directory contains the scripts dedicated to the statistical validation of the simulation model and the formal empirical testing of the research hypothesis.

These scripts fulfill **Specific Objective 4** of the thesis:
> *Validate the model by contrasting simulated procurement times against real historical records using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov (K-S) and Student's t tests at a significance level of α = 0.05, and test the dispersion hypothesis H1: σ_high > σ_low.*

---

## Workflow Overview

| Script | Title | Methodological Role |
|---|---|---|
| `08_model_validation.py` | Internal Diagnostics & Marginal Grouped-CV Evaluation | Separates internal phase-sum concordance, saved marginal OOF evidence and the absence of joint OOF validation; reports component-bootstrap uncertainty without IID simulation-versus-observation tests |
| `09_sensitivity_analysis.py` | Multi-Dimensional Robustness | Evaluates sensitivity to scenario cutpoints (P25/P75 vs. Tertiles), inter-stage copula dependence vs. independence, and yearly stability (2020–2025) |

## 1. Script 08 — Internal Diagnostics and Marginal OOF Evaluation

### Purpose and Evidence Scope
`08_model_validation.py` evaluates the unchanged 2,004 code-level historical records and the approved Script 07 trajectories while separating three evidence categories:

- **A — Internal diagnostics:** Concordance of historical published-schedule distributions and the correlated continuous phase sums simulated using full-cohort fitted models. This is internal fit/reproduction evidence.
- **B — Saved marginal OOF evaluation:** Grouped cross-validation predictions already generated by Script 05, distinguishing fixed candidates/ablations from `NESTED_SELECTED`. This is within-cohort, out-of-group evidence, not external or prospective validation.
- **C — Joint OOF validation unavailable:** Saved fold models do not contain joint copulas. Neither the independently calibrated TOTAL marginal nor the marginal phase predictions validate the joint phase sum outside training. No full-cohort copula is borrowed to manufacture OOF independence.

The earlier project-objective wording in this README does not describe tests executed by this implementation. There are no IID Welch/KS/Levene/Fligner contrasts between simulations and historical observations, automatic hypothesis decisions, p-value-based equivalence claims or arbitrary fit-quality labels.

### Contracts and Integrity
Explicit directory arguments locate the Script 05 nested-calibration artifacts, the `sixa-calibration-bundle/1.0` export, Script 07 simulation artifacts and a fresh output root. Required files must be listed in their manifests and pass SHA-256 checks before analysis. Historical IDs, joint profiles, scenarios, OOF cardinalities, outer/inner memberships, model-training identifiers and trajectory lineage are checked.

The historical target is `query_close_to_award_calendar_days`: differences between published administrative schedule dates, not verified actual event durations. Block A uses the continuous sum of absolution, integration and post-integration phases, matching Script 07. The separately fitted TOTAL marginal belongs only to the relevant Block B diagnostics.

Each of M1/M2/M4 is compared separately within low Q (476 records), medium Q (1,043) and high Q (485). No pooled global result is produced. Any later global comparison must use empirical scenario weights 476/2004, 1043/2004 and 485/2004 separately for each model, never simulation counts or pooled models.

### Diagnostics and Observational Uncertainty
- Reports observed and simulated means, medians, sample standard deviations, P90/P95 and differences **observed minus simulated** in days. Strict exceedances at 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120, 150 and 180 days use differences in percentage points.
- Computes exact empirical CDF supremum distances and Wasserstein distances as descriptive discrepancies without hypothesis-test p-values. The compact ECDF CSV contains values at observed knots; full supports are used for the exact distance calculations.
- Examines the three corresponding phases, including zero mass in integration. Only four historical integration intervals are positive; their empirical support remains limited and no model is refitted.
- Uses 2,000 whole-component bootstrap replicates, seed `2026110308`, sampling the 680 entity/project dependency components globally with replacement. All records in each selected component remain together across scenarios; the documented related families are not separated. Simulated distributions remain fixed and millions of trajectories are never bootstrapped as observational units.
- Pointwise 95% observational intervals and translated difference intervals are conditional on the fixed fitted model and available simulation. They exclude full calibration, selection and Monte Carlo uncertainty. Reporting requires at least 20 contributing components and at least 90% usable replicates; degenerate statistics are flagged without forced intervals. This is a support safeguard, not proof of representativeness.
- Block B reports MAE, predicted-minus-observed mean bias, P10–P90 coverage, lower/upper tail frequencies and available log-scores. Unsupported scores are counted and their mean is omitted. Government/year metrics require at least 20 records and 20 components; sparse cells retain counts and warnings with metrics suppressed.

Run the **ten synthetic tests** separately:


### Scientific Limits and Downstream Scope
Observed schedules are not actual completion durations, and simulated trajectories are not new procurement observations. National support is limited to 56 records/six components and FONAFE to one record/component; their subgroup metrics are suppressed by the reporting rule. Nominal amounts, historical selection, model dependence and parameter/selection uncertainty constrain generalization. There is no external, prospective or joint OOF predictive validation of the phase sum, and no causal conclusion.

Script 09 requires a separate audit for substantive sensitivities. It is not executed or approved here. The project statement and other unaudited sections of this README are preserved; this section documents only the implemented evidence and its limits.

## 2. Script 09 — Sensitivity and Robustness Analysis

### Purpose
`09_sensitivity_analysis.py` stress-tests the scientific conclusions across three alternative analytical dimensions to guarantee that findings are not artifacts of specific modeling choices.

### Sensitivity Dimensions
1. **Scenario Cutpoint Invariance:**
   - Primary scheme (P25 - P75: Q < 7 vs. Q > 35): Ratio = 1.73, Levene p = 1.48 × 10⁻²⁴.
   - Tertiles scheme (Low Q ≤ 10 vs. High Q > 27): Ratio = 1.75, Levene p = 6.65 × 10⁻²⁷.
   - Confirms that the dispersion effect is completely invariant to the partitioning convention.
2. **Temporal Subgroup Stability (2020 to 2025):**
   - Evaluates the variance ratio separately for each of the six study years.
   - In all six individual years, σ_high exceeds σ_low (H1 direction maintained = True; ratios ranging from 1.32 to 3.38).
3. **Copula vs. Independence Benchmark:**
   - Contrasts the full multi-stage Gaussian copula model against an independent stage assumption (Mean: 61.49 d vs. 61.46 d; P90: 113.6 d vs. 113.8 d), validating that copula dependence captures residual correlation without distorting total durations.

### Execution
From the repository root:
```bash
python 01_scripts/04_model_validation/09_sensitivity_analysis.py
```
