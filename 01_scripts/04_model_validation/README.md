# Internal Diagnostics, Marginal OOF Evaluation, and Sensitivity

This directory contains internal simulation diagnostics, marginal out-of-fold evaluation, and sensitivity analyses.

**Specific Objective 4**, as stated in the originally approved research project:

> *Validate the model by contrasting simulated procurement times against real historical records using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov (K-S) and Student's t tests at a significance level of α = 0.05, and test the dispersion hypothesis H1: σ_high > σ_low.*

---

## Workflow Overview

| Script | Title | Methodological Role |
|---|---|---|
| `08_model_validation.py` | Internal Diagnostics & Marginal Grouped-CV Evaluation | Separates internal phase-sum concordance, saved marginal OOF evidence and the absence of joint OOF validation; reports component-bootstrap uncertainty without IID simulation-versus-observation tests |
| `09_sensitivity_analysis.py` | Sensitivity analysis | Historical partitions, model comparisons, composition, annual descriptions, saved discrepancies and a paired residual dependence pilot |

## 1. Script 08 — Internal Diagnostics and Marginal OOF Evaluation

### Purpose and Evidence Scope
`08_model_validation.py` evaluates the unchanged 2,004 code-level historical records and the approved Script 07 trajectories while separating three evidence categories:

- **A — Internal diagnostics:** Concordance of historical published-schedule distributions and the correlated continuous phase sums simulated using full-cohort fitted models. This is internal fit/reproduction evidence.
- **B — Saved marginal OOF evaluation:** Grouped cross-validation predictions already generated by Script 05, distinguishing fixed candidates/ablations from `NESTED_SELECTED`. This is within-cohort, out-of-group evidence, not external or prospective validation.
- **C — Joint OOF validation unavailable:** Saved fold models do not contain joint copulas. Neither the independently calibrated TOTAL marginal nor the marginal phase predictions validate the joint phase sum outside training. No full-cohort copula is borrowed to manufacture OOF independence.

The earlier project-objective wording in this README does not describe tests executed by this implementation. There are no IID Welch/KS/Levene/Fligner contrasts between simulations and historical observations, automatic hypothesis decisions, p-value-based equivalence claims or arbitrary fit-quality labels.

### Contracts and Integrity
Explicit directory arguments locate the Script 05 nested-calibration artifacts, the `sixa-calibration-bundle/1.0` export, Script 07 simulation artifacts and a fresh output root. Required files must be listed in their manifests and pass SHA-256 checks before analysis. Historical IDs, joint profiles, scenarios, OOF cardinalities, outer/inner memberships, model-training identifiers and trajectory lineage are checked.

The historical target is `query_close_to_award_calendar_days`: differences between published administrative schedule dates, not verified actual event durations. Block A uses the continuous sum of absolution, integration and post-integration phases, matching Script 07. The separately fitted TOTAL marginal belongs only to the relevant Block B diagnostics.

Each of M1/M2/M4 is compared separately within low Q (476 records), medium Q (1,043) and high Q (485). No pooled global result is produced. Any later global comparison must use empirical scenario weights 476/2004, 1043/2004 and 485/2004 separately for each model, never simulation counts or pooled models.

### Diagnostics and Observational Uncertainty
- Reports observed and simulated means, medians, sample standard deviations, P90/P95 and differences **observed minus simulated** in days. Strict exceedances at 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120, 150 and 180 days use differences in percentage points.
- Computes exact empirical CDF supremum distances and Wasserstein distances as descriptive discrepancies without hypothesis-test p-values. The compact ECDF CSV contains values at observed knots; full supports are used for the exact distance calculations.
- Examines the three corresponding phases, including zero mass in integration. Only four historical integration intervals are positive; their empirical support remains limited and no model is refitted.
- Uses 2,000 whole-component bootstrap replicates, seed `2026110308`, sampling the 680 entity/project dependency components globally with replacement. All records in each selected component remain together across scenarios; the documented related families are not separated. Simulated distributions remain fixed and millions of trajectories are never bootstrapped as observational units.
- Pointwise 95% observational intervals and translated difference intervals are conditional on the fixed fitted model and available simulation. They exclude full calibration, selection and Monte Carlo uncertainty. Reporting requires at least 20 contributing components and at least 90% usable replicates; degenerate statistics are flagged without forced intervals. This is a support safeguard, not proof of representativeness.
- Block B reports MAE, predicted-minus-observed mean bias, P10–P90 coverage, lower/upper tail frequencies and available log-scores. Unsupported scores are counted and their mean is omitted. Government/year metrics require at least 20 records and 20 components; sparse cells retain counts and warnings with metrics suppressed.

Run the **ten synthetic tests** separately:

```bash
python 01_scripts/04_model_validation/08_model_validation.py --self-test
```

Tests cover input/manifest corruption, schema and ID/scenario/fold consistency, duplicates/missing records, known quantiles/distances/exceedances, distribution invariance to simulation replication, indivisible components, bootstrap reproducibility/degeneracy, known marginal OOF metrics, separation of evidence, warning propagation and protected publication failures. The real run also received an independent post-run audit of M1 low, M2 medium and M4 high, plus reproduction of bootstrap mean intervals.

### Main Outputs (`02_results/04_model_validation/`)
Paths are relative to the selected output root.

- `08_A_internal_diagnostics.json`: Nine model/scenario phase-sum and phase comparisons, observed/simulated statistics, exact distances, conditional observational intervals and simulation-precision warnings.
- `08_A_internal_differences.csv`: Observed-minus-simulated differences with explicit day/percentage-point conventions.
- `08_A_ecdf_at_observed_knots.csv`: Exact ECDF values at historical knots for reusable plotting; not a reduced grid used to approximate distances.
- `08_B_oof_marginal_evaluation.json`: Saved marginal OOF metrics and supported subgroup results; explicit declaration that joint phase-sum OOF validation is unavailable.
- `08_B_oof_metrics.csv`: The 27 model/outcome marginal OOF summaries, with null mean scores where support fails.
- `08_C_methodological_limits.json`: Evidence scopes and scientific warnings; no binary hypothesis decision.
- `08_run_manifest.json`: Input/code fingerprints, bootstrap seed/repetitions, versions and hashes/sizes of six analytical outputs. Staging verification precedes complete directory publication; existing outputs are rejected.

The run root additionally preserves execution records, pre-execution integrity hashes, post-run audit and an artifact inventory. Local results remain excluded from Git. No legacy binary hypothesis-decision artifact is generated.

### Scientific Limits and Downstream Scope
Observed schedules are not actual completion durations, and simulated trajectories are not new procurement observations. National support is limited to 56 records/six components and FONAFE to one record/component; their subgroup metrics are suppressed by the reporting rule. Nominal amounts, historical selection, model dependence and parameter/selection uncertainty constrain generalization. There is no external, prospective or joint OOF predictive validation of the phase sum, and no causal conclusion.

Script 09 requires a separate audit for substantive sensitivities. It is not executed or approved here. The project statement and other unaudited sections of this README are preserved; this section documents only the implemented evidence and its limits.

## 2. Script 09 — Sensitivity Analysis

### Stage 1 — Descriptive and structural sensitivity

The default mode reuses approved artifacts from Scripts 05–08 for the 2,004 procedures and 680 entity/project components. Q is the verified consultation and observation count. Durations describe published schedules.

- P25/P75 uses Q < 7, 7 ≤ Q ≤ 35, and Q > 35. Tertiles use Q ≤ 10, 10 < Q ≤ 27, and Q > 27. The historical high/low SD ratios are 1.728 and 1.752, respectively. With 2,000 whole-component bootstrap replicates, the pointwise 95% percentile intervals are [1.169, 2.653] and [1.280, 2.369].
- M1 and M4 are compared with M2 within each saved empirical scenario. Simulation budgets are metadata and do not weight scenarios. The historical M1-high individual SD precision warning remains (0.543690 days versus a 0.500000-day tolerance).
- Composition is reported by government level, nominal procedure amount in PEN, notice year and component. National Government and FONAFE have limited support. Annual comparisons cover 2020–2025, with the 2023 primary extreme contrast withheld for sparse support.
- Script 08 discrepancies retain the observed-minus-simulated sign for SD, P90, P95 and exceedances. Marginal out-of-fold evidence remains separate. Joint out-of-fold validation of the three-phase sum is unavailable. Scenario contrasts combine changes in Q and profile composition.

### Stage 2 — Paired residual dependence pilot

The explicit mode compares the approved Gaussian residual copula with an identity matrix constructed in memory. Both arms reuse Script 06, joint profiles, weights, calibrated marginal transformations, and integration treatment. Common random numbers share profile selection and base normals within each pair. The total is the continuous sum of the three phases. Shared profiles can induce marginal dependence under the identity copula.

The pilot used nine M1/M2/M4 × low/medium/high configurations, 30 independent replicates, two arms and 50,000 draws per arm and replicate, totaling 27 million realizations. The fixed seed is `202611030901` and the stream namespace is `residual_dependence_pilot_v1:`. The 13 contrasts per configuration use adjusted minus identity for mean, sample SD, median, linear P90/P95 and eight strict exceedances (30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120, 150 and 180 days). Exceedance differences are in percentage points.

| High Q model | SD change (days) | P95 change (days) |
|---|---:|---:|
| M1 | +0.51151 | +1.00337 |
| M2 | +0.02597 | +0.03242 |
| M4 | +0.03103 | +0.03447 |

All 117 contrasts met the Bonferroni half-width tolerances of 0.05 days for mean, 0.10 for SD and median, 0.25 for P90/P95, and 0.10 percentage points for each exceedance. The report also includes pointwise 95% t intervals and flags constant replicate differences separately. These intervals quantify Monte Carlo precision with fixed parameters at one N. Finite-sample SD and quantile bias, parameter uncertainty, and joint predictive validity remain unresolved. The pilot supports neither equivalence nor general invariance. No expansion is planned in this phase.

### Execution and contracts

Stage 1 checks exact provenance against the Script 08 manifest, including absolute input paths. Relocating a run can fail this check despite matching file hashes. Stage 2 requires the approved Stage 1 manifest at the repository-relative location given below. These are current contract limitations when moving the analysis to another machine.

Set the following shell variables to the approved local directories.
