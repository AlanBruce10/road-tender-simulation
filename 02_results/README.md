# Analytical Results

This directory contains the structured analytical outputs generated by the computational pipeline.

The files stored here are **derived research products**, not raw source data. They are generated sequentially by the scripts documented in [`01_scripts/`](../01_scripts/) and provide the numerical evidence, diagnostic results, methodological audits, stochastic-model outputs, validation evidence, and final synthesis used in the study.

> **Reproducibility principle:** files in this directory should be regenerated by executing the corresponding scripts rather than edited manually.

---

# Interpretation Boundaries

The result files should be interpreted according to the following principles.

### Empirical sample versus simulation

The empirical evidence base consists of the observed procurement procedures.

Monte Carlo iterations and DES entities are simulated realizations and do not constitute additional independent procurement observations.

### Primary versus sensitivity models

The primary stochastic architecture is established before final simulation.

Alternative scenario definitions, the Low Lognormal representation, and independent DES stage sampling are retained for robustness or sensitivity analysis rather than replacing the primary specification.

### Validation

Full-sample reproduction is treated as internal fidelity rather than independent validation.

Independent predictive assessment is performed using the later **2024–2025 temporal holdout**.

### Robustness

Robustness is evaluated conclusion by conclusion.

A conclusion may remain directionally robust even when particular numerical estimates are sensitive to model representation, process architecture, or temporal period.

### Causality

The study is observational.

The outputs characterize statistical association, stochastic behavior, predictive transportability, and robustness.

They do not establish a causal effect of consultation and observation volume on procurement duration.
